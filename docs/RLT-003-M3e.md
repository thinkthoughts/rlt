# RLT-003 · M3e — Complete modulo-30 classification: implementation report

**Scope.** M3e implements the architecture approved on 2026-11-09, with decisions D1–D8 as recommended. Architecture review: <https://claude.ai/artifact/STCqbkFdKRJZ8bxjrDC4qN>.

**Result.** RLT checks the classification of the residues below 30 as an unconditional theorem under ROBINSON-Q (E-T1):

```
∀r. r < 30 → (free235(r) → (r = 1 ∨ r = 7 ∨ r = 11 ∨ r = 13 ∨ r = 17 ∨ r = 19 ∨ r = 23 ∨ r = 29))
           ∧ ((r = 1 ∨ r = 7 ∨ r = 11 ∨ r = 13 ∨ r = 17 ∨ r = 19 ∨ r = 23 ∨ r = 29) → free235(r))
```

- **The biconditional.** NAT-DEF reads `→ ∧ ∨ ¬ ∀ ∃` and no `↔`, so the biconditional is written as its two implications (D2). The checked goal unfolds `<`, `∣` and `free235` to their registered bodies.
- **What E-T1 rests on.** It cites the two directions, E-FWD and E-BWD. Those cite:
  - M3d's enumeration DA01;
  - 22 positive divisibility facts;
  - 24 non-divisibility facts: 21 new, generated by M3c's scheme unchanged, and M3c's CA02–CA04 for 7.
- **Size and trust.** 49 cited-file checks, depth 3, inside the merged limits of 64 checks and 8 files. Every axiom used is one of the seven ROBINSON-Q axioms. The 20 inference rules and the citation mechanism are unchanged.

## 2. Proof dependencies

```
E_T1 ── FWD: E_FWD ── D: DA01 (M3d enumeration)
     │            └── P0, P2, …, P28: PD_k  (p ∣ k for the 22 non-representatives)
     └── BWD: E_BWD ── N2_r, N3_r, N5_r: ND_d_r for r ∈ {1, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29}
                       (r = 7: M3c CA02–CA04; the rest: tests/m3e/accept/ND_d_rr)
F_r (r ≠ 7): free235(r) from its three ND facts; F_7 is M3c's CA05.
EA_07: cites E_T1 and instantiates it at 7.
```

| File | Steps | Bytes | Cites | Cited-file checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E_T1 | 9 | 2,327 | E_FWD, E_BWD | 49 |
| E_FWD | 284 | 28,433 | DA01 and 22 PD | 23 |
| E_BWD | 80 | 7,159 | 24 ND | 24 |
| ND_2_29, the largest leaf | 469 | 29,575 | — | 0 |

**E-FWD.** Fix a, and assume a < 30 and free235(a).
1. DA01 at a gives a = 0 ∨ … ∨ a = 29.
2. 29 `or-cases` peel that disjunction.
3. **Representative cases** inject a = k into R₈(a).
4. **Non-representative cases:**
   - the cited PD fact p ∣ k is moved to p ∣ a by `subst` with motive (at u (dvd p u));
   - it meets the conjunct ¬(p ∣ a) of free235(a), giving ⊥;
   - `false-elim` concludes R₈(a).

**E-BWD.** Fix a, and assume R₈(a).
1. 7 `or-cases` peel R₈.
2. Each representative case builds free235(r) from its three cited ND facts, then moves it to a by `subst` with motive (at u (free235 u)).

**E-T1.** Instantiate both cited directions at a, then `and-intro`, `imp-intro` and `forall-intro`.

## 4. The theorem and the specification claim

- **E-T1 is a theorem.** It is a statement about natural numbers relative to ROBINSON-Q and the registered definitions.
- **The math29 link is a registered reading.** That the eight values are the representatives the math29 reading and RESIDUE-30 refer to stays a reading. CT3 records only that the theorem's list equals the record's pinned list.
- **Records unchanged.** RESIDUE-30's report still lists non-divisibility, gcd and completeness as "not established", because the record itself is unchanged. Connecting it to E-T1 is the `backed by` link milestone (D6), designed in the review's §9 and deferred.
