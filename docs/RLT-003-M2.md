# RLT-003 · M2 — Closed quotient–remainder witnesses modulo 30: implementation report

**Scope.** The work follows "RLT-003 M2 — Architecture Approved with Two Refinements". D1–D6 and D8–D9 are approved. D7 is replaced by the approved source-fidelity rule (§5). M2 is the only part implemented; M3 is not.

**Result.**
- RLT's kernel checks 111 closed equations under PEANO-ARITH with Γ = ∅.
  - 90 of them are division witnesses r = p × q + s, one for each r in 0..29 and each p in {2, 3, 5}.
  - 10 are remainder bounds s + (k + 1) = p.
  - 5 are class witnesses n = 30 × q + r, for n ∈ {29, 30, 31, 37, 59}.
  - 4 are bounds below 30.
  - The last two are the math29 example (1 + 1) × 3 × 5 = 30 and the tally 1 + … + 1 = 8.
- Under the registered convention RESIDUE-30, the registered classification of the 30 entries gives the eight representatives {1, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29}.
- That result is a **registered interpretation**, checked for consistency against the complete table. It is **not an RLT kernel theorem**.

## 1. Four kinds of claim, kept apart in every report

| Kind | Report label | What establishes it | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kernel-checked equation | `theorem … → ACCEPT — unconditional theorem relative to registered axiom set PEANO-ARITH` | the six-rule kernel, PEANO-ARITH, Γ = ∅ | `7 = (2 × 3) + 1`, `1 + (0 + 1) = 2` |
| Registered residue interpretation | `reading … — registered reading (not a theorem)` | the pinned convention RESIDUE-30 (rule `nonzero-remainders`) applied to bound witnesses | "remainders 1, 1, 2 (all nonzero): coprime representative of 30" |
| Consistency check on the complete table | `consistency … — consistency check, not a theorem` | the checker compares the classified table with the convention's registered representatives | the classification gives [1, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29] (8 of 30) |
| General claims awaiting proof capabilities | `not established …` | nothing yet (M3 and later) | uniqueness of remainders, non-divisibility, gcd(r, 30) = 1, completeness, congruence, periodicity, statements about primes > 5 |

The Lean comparisons (§6) are a fifth, separate kind: Lean-checked statements. They do not change any RLT label.

## 5. D7 as approved: source fidelity

- Reading-point records keep their original definitions and authority. M2 references `math29` by id (`references: [math29]`) and writes nothing to it.
- A new record may quote a definition accurately, with its exact source.
- The quotation must be a whole unit of a registered excerpt of that source. It is printed as `quotation "…" [SOURCE] defines math29; readingpoints/math29.yaml is unchanged (quoted; the definition stays with its source)`.
- An inaccurate, misattributed or redefining quotation is refused (S2, S3, S5, S6).

## 8. Independent adversarial audit (at 11e7be7) and follow-ups

An auditor with no part in the implementation worked on a fresh clone at 11e7be7.
- Its baseline was `cargo test` all passing, `verify.sh` with `RESULT all checks hold`, and the GDT comparison compiled.
- Verdict: conditional merge. No attack broke soundness: every witness that reached the classification had a kernel-checked equation and a matching bound, and no reading was printed with ACCEPT.

| # | Finding (severity) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Record `written` text was printed on the `theorem` line when binding failed before a goal existed, so a crafted string could show "→ ACCEPT …" inside an INVALID report (low) | Record text (`written`, basis, quotations, sources, paths) is printed quoted and escaped. The `theorem` label appears only for a goal that is accepted, unconditional, under PEANO-ARITH and bound; any other line is `refused`. |
| F2 | The GDT status line counted 8 statements; there are 7 (a comment matched `^theorem`) (low) | Count `^theorem `. |
| F3 | The CI job `gdt-comparison` could stay green when the status was unverified (no pipefail under `tee`) (low) | `shell: bash` with `set -o pipefail`. |
| F4 | `rlt residues` accepted a witness proved under PEANO-ADD, while the summary still said PEANO-ARITH (low) | Every witness, bound, example and tally must be under PEANO-ARITH; otherwise the record is refused. |
| F5 | The distinct-file count counted path spellings (`tests/m2/./B_1_2.rlt`) (low) | Canonical paths are counted. |
| Doc | §8 was a placeholder | This section. |

Hardening the audit suggested:

| Suggestion | Outcome |
|---|---|
| A `#print axioms` gate on tier 2 and on the GDT comparison | Added. A test file declaring `axiom cheat : False` gives `unverified — a statement depends on non-standard axioms: RLTGDTComparison.cheat`. |
| Refuse duplicate class witnesses, and constrain the example | Added. The example must be a closed product equal to the modulus. |
| Label conditional verdicts in INVALID reports | Added: they are labelled `refused`. |

Three issues the audit found predate M2 and live in shared input handling. They were fixed before the merge, in a focused hardening pass (`cli/tests/hardening.rs`):

| # | Issue | Fix | Regression tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | YAML alias expansion in the shared `load_yaml` could exhaust memory | Every record is read as a plain tree before it is built: YAML anchors, aliases and explicit tags are refused, in every record family | a 9²⁵-node alias file refused in under 2 s in `residues/`, `points/`, `readingpoints/` and `conventions/`; a single anchor; a tag `!!int "7"` |
| H2 | A symlinked `tests/` directory was followed by the shared `bind_proof` | Every component of a proof path below the root must be a real directory or file (`is a symbolic link or lies under one`) | symlinked `tests/` refused by `rlt residues` and by `rlt validate` for reading-point relationships; a symlinked subdirectory |
| H3 | Numerals spelled `0x07`, `0o7`, `+7` or `07` were read as 7 | A plain integer scalar must be decimal: `0`, or digits without a leading zero, optionally after `-` | each spelling refused with `numeral … is not decimal`; the spelling rule itself; quoted `"0x07"` stays text |

Valid inputs keep their meanings:
- H0 loads every committed record and checks that the hardened loader returns exactly the tree the unhardened loader returns.
- Run before and after the pass, the reports are byte-identical for all 215 proof files, `validate`, `rp math0/math5/math29`, both `points` records, `residues`, `chem` and every written-layer case.

Attacks that held:
- duplicate YAML keys;
- bad numerals (strings, negatives, 1001, 7.0, non-ASCII digits);
- swapped factors, q as an expression, a commuted sum, and every bound variant;
- remainder ≥ divisor;
- reuse of one proof for another claim;
- a class witness with the wrong divisor;
- conditional and assumption-laundered witnesses;
- a second partial residues file;
- a new residue-table convention (refused by the pin);
- changes to the representatives and the explanation (refused by the digests);
- symlinked records, directories and proof files;
- agreement between `validate` and `residues`;
- the quotation, checked against the source.
