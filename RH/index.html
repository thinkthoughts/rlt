Verified external development · November 21, 2026

On November 21, 2026, the Lean and Mathlib communities jointly certified the statement and proof in Levent Alpöge’s Lean formalization of the Riemann Hypothesis. This page records that external verification, not a new RLT proof or an independent replay by this site.

01 / Context

What the external verification establishes

The November 21 joint statement reports that Alpöge’s 17-million-line Lean library compiles, proves Mathlib’s existing RH statement, matches that canonical statement under independent comparison, and is accepted by the Lean kernel and additional checkers. This is a report by the Lean and Mathlib communities; RLT has not independently replayed this full development.
